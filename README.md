# Peek QR

A little see-through window that reads QR codes and text from your screen, your camera, or a picture. Free, with no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Everything is read on your own computer.

**Download:** https://matanestudios.com/apps/peek-qr

You can also grab the newest installer from [Releases](../../releases/latest).

## Windows "protected your PC" warning

Peek QR is not code-signed yet, so Windows may show a blue "Windows protected your PC" box the first time you run the installer. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
